LOCAL CHINESE RESTAURANT IN KENNEWICK, WA
JOIN US FOR YOUR NEXT MEAL
Welcome to Shang Hai Restaurant, a beloved Chinese dining destination in Kennewick, Washington, proudly serving the community since 2001. For over two decades, we’ve been dedicated to bringing you authentic, flavorful Chinese cuisine made with fresh ingredients and time-honored recipes. Our menu features a wide variety of traditional favorites, from savory stir-fries and comforting noodle dishes to crispy appetizers and flavorful Chinese specialties.

Whether you’re looking for a quick and delicious takeout meal or prefer to relax and enjoy a warm, inviting dine-in experience, we’re here to serve you. Our friendly staff is committed to providing exceptional service and a taste of China right here in Kennewick. Call us at (509) 783-8998 to place your takeout order, or stop by and savor the flavors of Shang Hai Restaurant today. We look forward to welcoming you!
