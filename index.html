MONTCIEL

Vallée de Joux, since 1851

Mont, the mountain. Ciel, the sky. Our name holds both: the valley a thousand metres up in the Jura where the maison was born, and the stars above it.

In 1851, Auguste Montciel set his first watch by the stars. One workbench in the Vallée de Joux, under a north window.

One watchmaker. One watch. From the first screw to the last. Every Montciel has been made this way for 175 years.

No two aventurine dials hold the same night sky. Platinum, 42 millimetres. One-minute tourbillon at six o'clock.

312 parts, each finished by hand, even those no one will ever see. Calibre MC 1851. 21,600 vibrations an hour. 72 hours of power reserve.

Tourbillon It turns once a minute, so gravity never finds the same flaw twice.

We found the whirlwind in the sky, and taught it to keep time.

You have spent a few seconds with Montciel. In that time the tourbillon has not yet completed its first turn, and its balance has beaten 0 times.

The Greeks had two words for time.

Chronos, the time that passes.

Kairos, the moment that stays.

We spend our lives mastering the first,

so that you may keep the second.
