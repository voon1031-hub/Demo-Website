    First time? Order like a regular.

    Chinese food is meant to be shared. Here is how a table of regulars orders.

    Share everything

    Order a few dishes for the middle of the table and pass them around. Plan on one dish per person, plus rice.

    Mix crispy, saucy and fresh

    Pick something crispy, something in a glossy sauce and something with vegetables. Fried shrimp, sweet and sour pork and chow mein is a classic trio.

    Start with a combination

    Not sure yet? A combination dinner puts three or four favorites on one plate, with soup. It's the easiest way to find yours. See the combination dinners

        The breath of the wok

        That smoky, toasty edge on good fried rice has a name in Cantonese: 镬气 wok hei. It comes from a blazing-hot wok and food that never stops moving.
