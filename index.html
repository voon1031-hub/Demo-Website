How a build runs with us

Five steps, with a fixed price agreed before anything is built.

  1. Site visit and brief

    We walk the land or building with you, listen to what you need, and check access, soil and utilities.

    You get: a written brief and a budget range.

  2. Design and approvals

    Our architect and engineer draw up the plans, and we submit them and follow them through the local authority.

    You get: approved drawings.

  3. Fixed quote and contract

    A priced bill of quantities, a programme with dates, and a contract written so you can read it.

    You get: one fixed price.

  4. Construction

    A supervisor on site every working day. Payments are tied to finished stages, not to dates.

    You get: photo updates every week.

  5. Handover

    We inspect the building together, fix anything on the list, and hand over the keys with every document in one folder.

    You get: keys, warranties and as-built drawings.
